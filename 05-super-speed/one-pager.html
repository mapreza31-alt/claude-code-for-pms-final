What's happening, in one paragraph

4.2 cut the ping wait to 60 seconds WIKI. The ranking code treats a missed ping exactly like a "no" (−0.12 each) and the score never recovers on its own CODE ANALYSIS. So a few early misses pushed Vesper, Farlight, The Undertow and Meteor Mite down the order, they stopped getting pings, and they had no way back. Their work moved to The Gale, Nightwell and Stormwrack ANALYSIS. Nobody on either side of the screen can see any of this. Kip has two cards side by side, one "dead quiet", one "on fire", and nothing that explains the gap WIKI.

The note in the code

This sits above record_declined() in history.py. It isn't signed; your message credits Wen CODE.

# TODO(2019): should this ease back toward NEUTRAL_SCORE on its # own after a while? For: somebody who had a bad month shouldn't still # be carrying it in the spring. Against: if somebody has stopped taking # work, we probably want that to stick until they take work again. # Leaving it as-is for now.

Both sides of her question are right, and they're about different people. "Stopped taking work" is someone saying no. "A bad month" is often someone who never got the chance to say yes. The code can't tell them apart, so it can't answer her. The data already can: turn-downs actually fell after 4.2 (21% → 18%), and the whole drop is misses ANALYSIS.

What we'd build instead

Two things people would notice. Each sits on a small change underneath, but the change is the plumbing, not the product.

For a handler like Kip

The quiet card explains itself

Meteor Mite · 2 pings this week (usually 9)
Moved down after 2 missed pings on 14 and 16 Aug. Coming back up: about 3 days.
  • The coverage card says why it's quiet in plain words, and when that will change. That answers Kip's ask: make it "look less like a coincidence" WIKI.
  • When a ping is live, the handler sees it too, with the seconds left. Dot and Ambrose both asked for this WIKI, and it's the first slice of the Q4 Handler phone app brief WIKI.
  • When a callout moves on, the card says what happened: "No answer in 60 s. Went to The Gale. Counted as missed, not a no."
For a responder who's gone quiet

A missed ping isn't a no

That one went to someone closer to ready.
Missing it doesn't count against you like a no. You're still near the top for Harborside.
  • The phone says plainly what a miss means, so Vesper isn't left standing in the kitchen with the phone and no idea what it cost him WIKI.
  • After a quiet stretch the phone says "You're back in rotation", because the score eased back. This is the "For" side of the 2019 note.
  • If someone keeps missing even when ranked first, the app runs a quiet check ("Tap if you got this") that counts for nothing. The four miss 75% of pings when first; others miss 16% ANALYSIS. We don't yet know if the phones are receiving.

What this keeps

  • The 60-second ping wait and the proximity weighting both stay. This isn't a revert. Wide-area responders asked for the ranking change, and reverting "just trades one angry group of responders for another" PRIYA.
  • Security Policy 4.1. Handlers see their own responders' place and reasons only, by cover name, never other responders' scores WIKI. We still need a check on whether storing scores by cover name is itself allowed ANALYSIS.
